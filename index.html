How do I find my rate?

Look at your electric bill. The simplest way: divide the total amount due for electricity by the kWh used that month. Example: $120 ÷ 800 kWh = $0.15 per kWh. Step-by-step guide.

    Where do I find the EnergyGuide label?

    It's the yellow label that says Estimated Yearly Energy Use in kWh. On an appliance you own, look inside the door or on the side or back (older units may no longer have it). For a new model, it's on the appliance in the store and usually in the store's online listing. You can also search the model number in the ENERGY STAR product finder. The kWh/year number is more accurate than our typical values, so use it when you can.
