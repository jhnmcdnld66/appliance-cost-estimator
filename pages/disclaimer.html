Disclaimer

All results are estimates. They are for general information only and are not a quote, bill, or guarantee from any utility.

  • Preset wattages are typical values. Your appliance may use more or less, so check its label or measure it with a plug-in usage meter.
  • The default electric rate is a placeholder based on an approximate U.S. average. Real rates vary by state, utility, plan, season, and time of day.
  • Appliances that cycle on and off (refrigerators, air conditioners, heaters, and others with thermostats) use less than their full rated power over time. The "percent of time running" field is a rough adjustment.
  • Estimates don't include fixed monthly charges, taxes that aren't part of your rate, or other costs like hot water used by a washer or dishwasher.

Don't rely on these numbers for financial, safety, or electrical decisions. Ask your utility or a licensed electrician for anything important.
