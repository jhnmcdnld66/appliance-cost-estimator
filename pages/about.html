About

The Appliance Running-Cost Estimator is a simple, free tool that shows roughly how much it costs to run common home appliances, from space heaters and air conditioners to EV chargers and light bulbs.

Pick an appliance, adjust the watts, hours, and your electric rate, and the calculator shows the estimated energy use and cost per hour, day, month, and year. Everything runs in your browser, and once loaded it works offline too.

This is a private beta built by John McDonald. Wattages are typical values and the default electric rate is a U.S. average placeholder, so for the best estimate, enter your own numbers. See the disclaimer for details.

Open the calculator

Results are estimates, not a quote from your utility. No data is collected.
