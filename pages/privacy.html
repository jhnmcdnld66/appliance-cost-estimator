Privacy

We don't collect any data.

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or forms that send information anywhere.
  • No cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, or ads.
  • All calculations happen in your browser. The numbers you type never leave your device.
  • The app saves its own files in your browser's cache so it can work offline. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

Links to other sites. If you tap "Share on X" or another outside link, you leave this site, and that website's own privacy policy applies. The share button only opens X with suggested text; nothing is posted unless you choose to post it.

If this policy changes, for example if affiliate links are added, this page will be updated first.
